# Stage IV – Ascension

**Status: Direction and boundaries only.** This stage contains the ultimate reveal and the Universal Ascension Protocol. No implementation-level mechanics are decided here.

## Goal

Make ascension a durable, mechanically significant choice and reveal the larger EPOCH context only in this final stage.

## Required systems

- EPOCH-specific research development, traditions, Ascension Perks, buildings, and strategic infrastructure.
- Consequential, clearly differentiated routes for organic empires, Hive Minds, and Machine Intelligences.
- Durable effects on planets, economy, military, and existing EPOCH systems.
- Player-driven, staged healing of negative EPOCH consequences accrued in earlier stages. Healing requires real actions, costs, prerequisites, decisions, or projects; stage entry alone never cures them.
- Final revelation of the wider system and Universal Ascension Protocol belongs here.

## Boundaries and open decisions

This is not an automatic cleanup pass or free technology grant. Define class-specific prerequisites, research/tradition/perk unlocks, infrastructure placement and upkeep, healing eligibility/costs, failure and recovery, and final outcomes in a separate implementation design. Preserve existing feature family rules and real economic effects. No detailed research tree, tradition list, building effect, or UAP outcome is binding until that design is reviewed.

## New Stage-III boundary and prerequisites

Stage III is a **single universal crisis** shared by participating, nonparticipating and opted-out empires. Its public galactic resolution does **not** automatically open Stage IV for everybody and must not grant personal Stage-I/II science, final consent or healing rewards.

Personal Stage-IV eligibility remains conditioned on **an explicitly continued Stage-II personal storyline** and any later earned requirements. A permanent personal refusal continues to bar private Stage-IV narrative access even if the empire suffered, fought, or helped end the universal Nullwarden crisis. The detailed handling of Stage-II nonparticipants who win the galactic war is not a backdoor ascension: they can receive generic defense research and victory acknowledgement only.

The global crisis leaves real physical consequences on worlds and systems. Future Stage-IV healing can address eligible damage through paid, player-driven actions; Stage-III completion itself does not wipe the three existing feature families or temporary/permanent empire-class penalties.
