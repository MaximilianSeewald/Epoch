# Stage III – Military Crisis

**Status: Direction and boundaries only.** This is a distinct stage after Stage II's fifth chapter, not an extension or finale within Stage II. Detailed mechanics remain open.

## Goal

Escalate the Nullwarden response into a substantially larger military crisis. Prior player choices affect Nullwarden adaptation, threat, intelligence, and available defenses. Convergence is a galaxy-scale threat, not an abstract progress bar.

## Required experience

- Real hostile fleets and actual combat outcomes processed by Stellaris.
- Threatening system and planetary conditions that affect play.
- Strategic decisions, preparation and defense, active alternatives, retreat, and lasting consequences.
- Clear connection to Stage II outcomes and meaningful carry-forward state.
- Recoverable routes that maintain campaign agency after losses.

## Boundaries and dependencies

Stage III owns adapted Nullwardens, a larger convergence threat, and the extended military escalation. It must not be reduced to fleet illustrations in events or artificial progress counters. It inherits the shared empire-local state, ownership/scope validation, empire-class support, voluntary exit, and save/load/MP requirements. Exact fleet sizes, spawning/ownership model, war/hostility rules, objective structure, system/planet mechanics, alternatives, and ending/handoff are open design decisions. They require a dedicated technical design and target-version verification before implementation.
