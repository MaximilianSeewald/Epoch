# Stage II – Resonance

**Status: Implemented for gameplay acceptance; all new engine behavior remains untested in game.** Exactly five connected chapters. [Technical evidence](../technical-contract.md), [acceptance](../testing.md), and [migration](../architecture/content-migration.md) distinguish source/static evidence from runtime results.

## Entry and persistent state

Stage I's continuation option starts a country-local clock: 1,800–1,960 days (Stellaris years use 360 days). The timed flag and scheduled event survive independently of any planet. Yearly recovery starts an invitation only after that timer expires. Permanent refusal blocks entry. Already scheduled pre-entry saves retain their original timer; saves without one receive the full interval at their first recovery pulse because the original decision date cannot be reconstructed safely.

`epoch_res_schema_v1`, `epoch_res_started`, `epoch_res_active` and integer `epoch_res_chapter` (1–5) identify the new campaign. Callback guards check chapter, owner and the specific result target. Only native successful projects, the native military destruction hook and completed planet decisions advance objectives. The five-stage Resonance situation has zero monthly progress and no automatic stage dispatch. No calendar timer completes an objective. Eight required staffed-science-ship investigations plus optional reconnaissance, combat and several planet actions provide the active content; the target of 1.5–2 times Stage I's active play must be measured during gameplay acceptance.

Planet/object targets are keyed by campaign country. Scientific completion explicitly forwards the project location as `from`, while callbacks resolve targets with `@root.owner`. Infection dispatch enters a fresh country event before using country-rooted setters. The three shared feature families and permanent Stage-I penalties remain attached to their real worlds.

## Military proof and bounded threat

The `on_fleet_destroyed_victim` hook documents This = victim country, From = opposing country, FromFrom = destroyed fleet. `epoch_res.201` requires the dedicated Nullwarden country, its flagged destroyed fleet with matching owner, its persisted campaign-owner link and active Chapter 2. Direct campaign kills qualify. For an allied finish, the exact destroyed fleet must carry a participation record from its current engagement and the matching fleet-keyed system. `epoch_res.200` checks Root/From and the optional FromFrom/FromFromFrom fleets named by the native join callback; a country-wide historical flag or an unrelated nearby fleet cannot supply proof. The success callback never samples a surviving ally's `is_in_combat`.

`on_fleet_exit_battle` marks the engagement ended and schedules disposal one day later, retaining evidence across same-day exit/destruction dispatch in either order. A subsequent join synchronously clears the previous participation before recording the new fight; disposal does not erase a fresh ongoing engagement. Flight/exit/disposal never grants success, cleanup markers block success, and the chapter transition prevents duplicate completion. Vanilla documents the scopes and a per-fleet exit hook, but not their exact ordering or a usable battle identifier. The one-day disposal and native join coverage therefore remain explicit runtime assumptions, including allied final blows, save/load and same-day consecutive battles.

Recovery validates target existence, scope type and campaign identity before use. A valid reciprocal owner link can backfill independent country/fleet identity flags. If primary references are lost, a recovery-only country/fleet search uses those identities to reacquire the original; foreign campaigns are never adopted. A living original blocks replacement even if its primary target or spawn flag is lost. Absence must be established before the paid option appears and rechecked when selected; payment, the once-only allowance and the pending replacement are committed together. A missing country can be recreated only with independently verifiable absence. An ambiguous pre-fix save without primary or independent identity fails closed and cannot claim victory or create another force.

Scale is sampled once: four baseline tier-2 corvettes; two more if age >25 years, destroyer technology or an owned fleet >800 power; two more only if age >60, cruiser technology and an owned fleet >1,800 power. Bounds are four/eight, without ongoing escalation. Display power uses actual designs, not fabricated overrides. No strategic fleet AI, colony bombing, reinforcement loop or empire-wide pursuit is enabled. Vanilla guardian countries demonstrate AI-disabled tactical fleets; the diplomatic/technology/event modules, non-neutral faction and aggressive return-point controls are deliberately supplied. Fairness and pursuit limits remain balance/runtime checks.

## Planetary containment

| Decision | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Quarantine | 200 energy, 100 minerals | Production −10%, stability +2 until resolution; a non-unstable feature becomes latent. It cannot cheaply reset an unstable site. |
| Localize | 150 energy | Maps infected districts; prerequisite for sampling, extraction and either finish. |
| Sample | 250 energy | Once per site. Quarantine or the researched infrastructure building produces clean data and 150 society research. Unsecured sampling instead makes the feature unstable and adds 10 devastation. |
| Exploit | 100 energy | Once per site: 300 physics research, 100 alloys; local physics output +15% and stability −3 for one year; unstable feature and 10 devastation. |
| Isolate districts | 350 energy, 200 minerals | Requires quarantine and instability; returns to latent, adds 5 devastation and permits careful stabilization. |
| Stabilize | 300 energy, 100 alloys | Requires localization, quarantine and non-unstable state; controlled feature, quarantine removed, successful end state. |
| Emergency containment | 700 energy, 200 alloys | Requires localization; controlled feature but 20 devastation and permanent production −5% / stability −3. Records failure and continues. |

All measures take 60–90 days of actual planet decision work and recheck ownership, chapter and action prerequisites on completion. At least localization plus a finishing action is required; the economical route needs quarantine as well. Research/exploitation/isolation provide additional tactical routes. An unstable infection remains manageable through isolation or emergency shutdown. No research roll is required to finish.

The four Stage-II feature states retain inherited Stage-I class penalties once. Their own class-specific modifiers affect organic food, Hive unity or Machine energy: −1% latent, −3% resonant, −6% unstable, +1% controlled, alongside the existing common research/stability/production effects. Feature setters clear only contamination; scar and structure families remain independent.

## Research, findings and later-stage boundary

Spectroscopy is a normal tier-1 physics research option (+3% national physics); it unlocks the Resonance Laboratory (400 minerals, 3 energy upkeep, +5% local physics). Neutral Infrastructure is a normal tier-2 society option requiring Spectroscopy (+3% national society); its building costs 500 minerals/3 energy upkeep, gives +5% local society and +2 stability, and secures sampling without quarantine. Both leads are offered; neither is granted researched, neither gates campaign completion. Spectroscopy plus entirely local wreck/network science earns a precise network map. Controlled pulses add 150 physics after real reflection analysis; dangerous repetition adds 300 physics after suppression analysis but burdens national energy by 2% for five years. Quiet observation avoids those costs and exposure. Research quality, exposure and planet/battle outcomes persist for later stages.

Adaptive Armor, Phased Sensors, Field Control, Chamber, Countermark, traditions and ascension perks have no active Stage-II path. Stage II stops at Chapter 5 and preserves uncertainty about the network's purpose.

## Save, AI and multiplayer contract

New games, Stage-I saves and post-Stage-I/pre-entry saves use the new schema. All state lives on countries/planets, not transient ship instances. Lost scientists/ships do not count as project success. Restore objectives in the situation or the capital decision reopens the current task; paid fallback routes keep native science participation. The AI gets the same projects, decisions, costs and military victory condition, with owner-local yearly retry of missing objectives and native decision weights. No AI victory flag or galaxy-wide periodic scan exists.

Prototype Stage-II saves are explicitly suspended, never marked successfully migrated. Their old queued callbacks are inert receivers; retired project keys abort immediately; the retired Chamber situation is a zero-progress compatibility shell cleaned by migration. Legacy persistent consequences remain defined/localized for saves but have no active writers. The new handoff is not issued to incompatible saves. Fresh game, save/load, parallel empires, AI and MP behavior all await gameplay acceptance.
