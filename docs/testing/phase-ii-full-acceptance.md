# Stage II Acceptance Plan

**Status: Replacement acceptance contract for the five-chapter design.** This is a plan, not a completed test report. The former eight-chapter test script is superseded and its historical results remain unchanged.

## Chapter gates

1. **The Scar Wakes:** confirm the entry preserves each Stage I outcome; complete a real Science Ship project with valid target and scientist or the documented paid fallback; compare risk, findings, and economics across Stage I outcomes. Time alone must not resolve it.
2. **The First Hunters:** confirm a hostile Nullwarden fleet exists in a visible system and Stellaris treats it as a real fleet. Exercise combat victory, diversion, prepared defense, and costly withdrawal on separate saves. Confirm fleet/combat state and durable chapter outcome correspond. Popup choice or time passage alone must not resolve the chapter.
3. **The Broken Code:** complete wreck/signal and archive fallback projects with proper science involvement, target, costs, callbacks, and persistent research leads. Confirm no technology is granted automatically. Exercise cancellation, loss, and retry.
4. **The Second Infection:** test quarantine, research, exploitation, and failure on equivalent planetary saves. Verify distinct actions/costs, immediate economic and stability effects, one state per feature family, and recovery after failure. Verify no feature becomes manually clearable and no softlock occurs.
5. **The Network of Silence:** complete projects at multiple actual systems with concrete marked targets. Compare branches after differing earlier decisions. Verify a durable Stage III handoff and no sixth Stage II chapter/event.

## Cross-cutting acceptance

Repeat across organic, Hive Mind, and Machine Intelligence empires; two simultaneous independent empires; player and AI; fresh saves and prototype-era saves; English and German; single player, host/client multiplayer; and save/load at every chapter boundary. Verify foreign-target isolation, no duplicate project/reward/fleet, valid fallback when target/ship/scientist is missing, economic tooltip accuracy, no Stage I regression, and clean fresh logs. Inspect real in-game UI and log output.

Report game build, mod commit, empire class, route, save checkpoints, screenshots, and log findings. Distinguish automated static/source checks from in-game results. A static pass is never an in-game acceptance claim. Resolve mechanics and expected numeric outcomes in the test case before using it as a pass/fail gate.
