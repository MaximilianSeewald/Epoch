# ADR-002: Stage-III Integration, Safety and Evidence Policies

> **Current contract, 2026-11-09:** The post-playtest implementation request supersedes the opening-only scope and earlier pre-implementation approval gates. The connected five-chapter campaign is implemented for static review; new gameplay has **not** been run in Stellaris. The user's first test confirms only the previous opening at `b421408`. All resolution variants now require destruction of every registered fortified node, the unique command ship in actual combat, and surviving hostile formations. Science weakens logistics and combat; it never withdraws, deletes or automatically defeats the command formation. Cooperation uses this same military validator. See [current implementation](../phases/stage-iii-full-implementation.md).


**Status: normative planning contract; no gameplay implementation or runtime acceptance.** Date: 2026-11-08. Refines [ADR-001](adr-001-galactic-crisis.md) and resolves the alternatives in the [archived analysis](../research/stage-iii-analysis-2026-11-08.md). ## Authority and unchanged mechanics

The [specification](../phases/stage-iii-military-crisis.md) states the experience, this ADR defines integration edge cases, the [implementation plan](../phases/stage-iii-implementation-plan.md) defines AP0–AP11 and G1, the [engine contract](../phases/stage-iii-engine-contract.md) defines proofs, the [evidence table](../phases/stage-iii-engine-evidence.md) records actual evidence, and the [acceptance matrix](../phases/stage-iii-acceptance.md) defines tests. They are the current contract. Historical research records observations and proposals, not additional competing implementation rules. New contrary evidence must update these linked contracts together before changing behavior.

Stages I/II retain their costs, clocks, callbacks, projects, features, decisions, tactical enemy and permanent personal refusal. Stage III reads `epoch_res_complete`, `epoch_res_stage_iii_ready` and the opt-out state; it does not write those facts, fabricate old outcomes or reuse personal event targets for global state. No refactor or modification of Stage-I/II mechanisms is part of Stage III. A discovered incompatibility that cannot be isolated in new Stage-III code blocks that work and requires a separately reviewed scope decision.

## I01 — Physical planet state and active personal objectives

Existing Contamination, Scar and Silent Structure families keep at most one visible state each. Preserve contained/controlled/healed, completed studied outcomes, paid actions, permanent damage and independent family state. Apply existing contamination deposits on conflict-free eligible carriers, with Stage-III-owned identities and validated economy. Do not call old infection/mission setters as a global exposure API: they clear shared state, reference personal targets and may dispatch rewards or recovery.

Avoid family transitions on any active Stage-I/II objective, including an in-progress investigation whose result is not yet saved. Do not infer protection merely from one flag; AP4 must derive a read-only protected-target predicate from existing canonical targets and saved state. For a protected, already occupied, or active objective world where no safe family transition exists, use a real supplementary Stage-III crisis modifier instead. It is not a fourth visible feature family and must not remove old effects. This exception applies even to a one-colony uninitiated empire. Thus E03 means a new contamination feature **where conflict-free eligible**; I01 takes priority over forcing a feature everywhere.

New Stage-III operations and countermeasures may reuse deposit definitions but must not set old campaign progression/mission flags or use old callbacks to unlock actions. Validate every minimal carrier flag required by a reused deposit against old migration and recovery; otherwise use the supplementary modifier until proven safe. Do not assume old setters are a side-effect-free public API. After any subsequent old callback, Stage III must preserve its own genuine pressure without overriding the callback's original result. Physical damage stays with a transferred planet; private projects, consent and rewards stay with their country.

## I02 — Cohorts, universal exposure and intake

All ordinary spacefaring empires include normal subjects/vassals, biological empires, Hives, Machines, genocidal variants, AI and humans. Sovereignty is not an exclusion filter. Playable colonyless/mobile countries require a valid fleet/system/economic effect instead of an invalid colony/capital access. The crisis country excludes itself. Observer and special-country treatment is explicit in the evidence matrix, not an accidental `default`-only assumption.

Freeze the initial cohort at first **public** enrollment at actual outbreak or later country appearance; private preparation offers do not freeze it: Prepared requires completed Stage II, explicit Continue, no opt-out and actual paid pre-M preparation success; Experienced requires the same personal eligibility without finished preparation; Uninitiated covers everyone else. Later research can improve defenses but cannot rewrite the initial history or award retroactive warning. First exposure has a bounded per-country quota and per-physical-world exposure-generation record. A conquest, subject change or secession is not permission to reapply the same outbreak damage to an already exposed planet. A new country is enrolled once, without inheriting private history or creating a second crisis. Later colony founding never restarts the initial quota; subsequent spread requires a living physical source, bounded target rules and its own cap.

Measure **additional** crisis harm against matched pre-outbreak economies within each empire class, including research gains as well as output/stability losses. Prepared must still suffer material nonzero impact, and comparable Uninitiated must suffer strictly greater aggregate initial impact. For Experienced, target an intermediate impact and verify it in AP4/AP9. Icon count or a penalty to a resource with no production is not evidence of harm. Existing permanent penalties do not count as new crisis damage.

## I03 — Clock and initialization compatibility

Use the configured M. Candidate comparisons `mid_game_years_passed >= -20`, `< 0`, and `>= 0` have source precedent, not a Stage-III runtime pass. Prefer a cheap global monthly guard plus the midgame hook, with bounded recovery; never use a fixed 2280/2300 date. AP1 verifies offsets, dispatch cadence and the setting's numeric units before any manual arithmetic. Preparation access and success both recheck eligibility; no retroactive rewards after M. Read the existing `epoch_res_final_choice_v1` proof and reject `epoch_res_final_choice_pending` as well as opt-out, unsupported/suspended personal state and incomplete/absent ready records. Current main writes the v1 marker before the final choice and ready only on Continue; older unproven ready flags must never infer consent. Do not change the old migration to make a country eligible.

Version-1 support target: new games, compatible old saves first initialized before M, and saves already carrying a supported Stage-III schema. An old save first initialized between P and M receives only the remaining preparation window. A first installation into a save already at/after M is **not supported for automatic crisis activation in v1**. Fail closed with a diagnosable initialization reason; do not ambush the galaxy, silently invent pre-warning or mark the crisis won. Any future opt-in/grace-period migration is a separate design and runtime gate. Existing initialized III saves resume normally after M; a load is not a first installation. Unknown newer schemas are not reset. Prototype Stage-II suspension remains intact and grants no private preparation; public crisis exposure is independent of that history.

## I04 — Safe fronts, reachable objectives and bounded retry

Target outbreak window is M..M+1 when safe placement is possible. Search only bounded valid candidates, reject capital ambushes, foreign-asset replacement and inaccessible objectives. Player/science access and enemy access are separate checks. Small maps may combine three functions on two physical stations. Never fabricate a destroyed/secured function to meet the start window.

If no valid topology can be placed, retain one reserved pending instance, retry at bounded regular intervals, expose a diagnostic reason and permit delayed activation beyond M+1. There is no unconditional timing guarantee in an impossible topology. Version 1 does not generate extra systems or seize a foreign starbase as a fallback. AP3 must establish a safe placement predicate; AP10 must test saturated/disconnected maps. Per-attempt work remains capped; waiting for a valid topology creates neither fleets nor victory. Missing/inaccessible objectives during the war use bounded owner-safe recovery and genuine replacements or alternate physical targets; replacement must not duplicate a surviving canonical object.

## I05 — Authoritative state without an invented host command

One global simulation lifecycle, not one instance per country or UI client. Source-supported global flags/targets plus a validated persistent carrier hold canonical facts. Do not assume `is_host`, `set_global_variable`, a save-persistent no-scope numeric variable, or transactional event dispatch exists. AP2 chooses actual supported storage and proves its behavior. The original eligible country is never the permanent owner of the global clock.

Use phases dormant → preparation window → activation reserved → active fronts → convergence → resolving → resolved, plus an explicit diagnostic recovery state. Set reservation before creating objects; each substep records completion and is idempotent. Persist schema, activation identity, budgets, object identities/generations, function transitions and terminal tombstone. Unexpected carrier death is not victory. Recover references from trustworthy object identity; if remaining evidence is insufficient, stop unsafe dispatch and diagnose rather than recreate a whole live crisis.

The SP-only load hook cannot be MP recovery. Verify additive on-action registration, normal simulation recovery, simultaneous dispatches and host/client reload. No local UI event is the sole authority. Termination first prevents future spawns and repeated payouts, then cleans only proven Stage-III-owned objects. Cleanup-generated deaths are explicitly excluded from military objective credit.

## I06 — Strategic controller, occupation scope and budget

Create a separate Stage-III country type; never modify or repurpose Stage II's `epoch_nullwarden`. Preferred research direction: bounded scripted strategy using native fleet travel/combat orders, compared against a custom type using Vanilla Berserk AI. AP3 decides after real path/attack/retreat/save tests; do not run competing strategic controllers on the same fleet. Native minister names are not proof of freely reusable behavior, and internal construction/spawns must not bypass the budget.

Version-1 baseline promises real mobile raids, battles, starbase/installation threat and validated system/planetary pressure. It does **not** promise ground occupation. Do not use direct scripted ownership transfer as a substitute for a claimed invasion, or describe an occupied planet when only a hostile fleet is present. Ground landing/occupation is an explicitly gated future extension requiring transport, escort, landing, ground combat, ownership/control and reconquest proofs. This preserves the ZIP engine contract's permitted physical-threat alternative.

Anchor research preference is an immobile armed station-ship in its own fleet, analogous to the Gray Tempest factory; immobility, map visibility, destruction and scientific interaction remain AP3 proofs. A flag or ambient object alone is not a destroyable station. Distinguish physical objects from their three assigned functional roles. One physical loss may disable several assigned functions exactly once.

Initial **probe parameters**, not approved production balance: at most four physical anchors; twelve active mobile combat fleets; one unique commandant outside that mobile count; twenty-four lifetime mobile spawn operations including initial forces/replacement. Reserve the commandant/end-objective budget separately. Record per-source subbudget, cooldown, active count and total spent. Splitting, merging, retreat, MIA or fleet destruction never restores lifetime budget. Anchor replacement is separately capped and never resets the front's source budget. Actual production numbers require AP9 measurements and an explicit parameter table before release.

Reinforcement callbacks recheck living source, current phase and budget at execution, even when scheduled earlier. No time-based victory. Exhausted budgets must leave a physical endgame accessible; never demand kills that cannot occur. Restrict pathfinding to a few candidates per replan, not whole-galaxy daily searches; `can_access_system` is documented expensive. Store chosen targets and replan on defined loss/blockage/idle/retreat events.

## I07 — Shared function validator and truthful national credit

Military breakthrough requires every registered function disabled and the real unique commandant destroyed. Science-supported military victory requires real node destruction before access to physical research sites. One study restricts logistics; two weaken surviving weapons/shields. Every registered node, the unique command ship and surviving hostile formations must still be destroyed in combat. Scientific withdrawal is prohibited. Combined-function stations on small maps must still provide two genuine research locations.

Joint containment is a cooperation mode over those same physical shutdown/destruction requirements, with independent proven contributions by at least two empires, not a third timer/counter-only completion predicate. Solo/isolated empires retain military and science-supported military solutions. A common validator owns global resolution; country rewards are independently guarded.

Track active, contested, destroyed, scientifically disabled and unresolved-reference states separately. Missing targets never satisfy a function. Native victim-destruction scopes, actual participant/target identity and cleanup reason are mandatory. `on_space_battle_won` includes exits/disengagement and is not sufficient. Do not infer the unique commandant ship's death from a different escort or entire-fleet reference. Coalition recognition can be conservative; do not invent damage percentages or shared kills when only the final blow is proven. All world scars, private refusal and Stage-IV prerequisites survive global victory.
